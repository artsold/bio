# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: UA and creative leads at mobile game studios (casual, mid-core, shooters) who are hiring a remote UA Creative / Motion Designer. They open the site from a CV link, LinkedIn or a referral, usually between other candidates, and decide within a minute or two whether the ads are good enough to bring the person to an interview. They know the vocabulary (hooks, IPM, CPI/ROAS, Tier-1, A/B tests) and judge by the ads themselves.

Secondary: recruiters and HR who forward the profile; they need the role, experience, location and the CV quickly.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Artiom Soldatov, Motion Designer & AI Creative Producer. Its job is to get the visitor to watch the ads and then reach out (email, LinkedIn) or download the CV. Success: a UA lead watches at least one ad with sound and contacts him.

## Positioning

Full-cycle ownership by one person: concept → copy (ad script) → final video. He builds AI production pipelines (LLM scripts and storyboards, assets in Seedream / Flux Kontext, animation in Kling / Seedance, finish in After Effects) and his ads ran in paid rotation for Nordcurrent titles. The confirmed lead message is the full cycle; the AI pipeline and paid-rotation results support it.

## Product Principles

1. The ads are the proof; the visitor should be watching one within seconds.
2. Show the full cycle, not a list of tools: concept, copy and video come from one person.
3. Plain claims only: every number on the page is one he can stand behind in an interview.
4. Respect a hiring lead's time: one clear path to watch, then one clear path to contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: readable contrast, keyboard access to every ad, reduced-motion respected, light enough to load on a phone connection.
