# Surface: souldatov.com home (index.html)

Mode: experience (portfolio). Audience: UA / creative leads at casual and hyper-casual mobile game studios. Job: watch the ads, then email, LinkedIn or download the CV.

Scope: re-skin of the existing page. Content, section order, carousel, lightbox, CV links, GA4 events and copy stay exactly as they are. Only the visual wrapper changes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The same page and the same casual-game identity, made quieter so the ads lead: three grounds instead of five, the outlined Lilita lettering reserved for the name and the closing call. It refuses the five-colour carnival where every heading, number and label shouts at the same volume.

OWN-WORLD: Flame #FF4B12 ground for the hero and the contact close, gold #FFD700 for the reading sections (About, Education), navy #1A1A2E for Work and Experience as one continuous dark stage. Lilita One for display, Nunito for everything else (no monospace). Chunky pressable buttons with the navy drop edge, rounded 20px ad cards, navy marquee band with white type and gold dashes.

STORY: The lead reads the name, the status and the eyebrow line, sees the face, can grab the CV; scrolls into a calm dark stage of ads, opens one with sound; reads the experience on the same stage; finishes on a flame close with the outlined call and the email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Unchanged composition. Status pill top left, eyebrow line in Nunito 800 at 19px sentence case, ARTIOM / SOLDATOV in Lilita with the navy outline, round photo right, gold Download CV button under the name; stat row below with 52px navy numerals and sentence-case Nunito labels. No rule under the name.

FORM: re-skin of the incumbent page, variant "Чище" (2 of 2 shown). seed: none (pinned) — "Я хочу оставить как можно ближе к существующей цветовой гамме"; "Чище".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
